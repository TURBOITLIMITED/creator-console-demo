Telegram Accounts

Connect New Account

To connect a Telegram account:

  1. Go to @BotFather and create a new bot (or use existing)
  2. Enable Secretary Mode: /mybots → your bot → Bot Settings → Secretary Mode
  3. On your personal account: Settings → Business → Chatbots → add the bot
  4. Grant reply permission when prompted
